Cookie Policy

Last updated: May 6, 2025

This Cookie Policy explains how TD Media ("we," "us," or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on tdmedia.io. This policy should be read alongside our Privacy Policy.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, improve functionality, and provide analytical information to website owners. Cookies can be "session cookies" (deleted when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them).

2. Similar Technologies

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies such as:

  • Web beacons / pixels: small transparent images embedded in web pages or emails to track user behaviour and measure campaign performance.
  • Local storage: browser storage used to save data locally on your device for functionality purposes.
  • Scripts: code that collects information about how you interact with our website.

3. Categories of Cookies We Use

3.1 Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are essential for the website to function properly. They enable core features such as page navigation and access to secure areas. The website cannot function without these cookies, and they cannot be disabled.

3.2 Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies collect information about how visitors use our website, such as which pages are visited most often and how users navigate the site. This information is used in aggregate form to improve how our website works. We use analytics tools such as Google Analytics for this purpose.

3.3 Functional Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make (such as language preference) and provide enhanced, more personal features.

3.4 Marketing & Targeting Cookies

These cookies are used to deliver advertisements and content more relevant to you and your interests, and to measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. They may be set by us or by third-party advertising partners such as Meta (Facebook) and Google. These cookies track your browsing activity across websites.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website are set by third parties. We have limited control over these cookies. The relevant third parties' privacy and cookie policies govern their use:

  • Google Analytics: Google Privacy Policy
  • Meta Pixel: Meta Privacy Policy
  • Google Ads: Google Ads Settings

6. Your Cookie Choices

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject non-essential cookies. You can manage your cookie preferences in the following ways:

  • Cookie banner: When you first visit our website, a cookie consent banner will allow you to accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your preferences at any time.
  • Browser settings: Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website. For guidance, visit allaboutcookies.org.
  • Opt-out tools:
    • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
    • Meta ads: Facebook Ad Preferences
    • Interest-based advertising: YourOnlineChoices (EU)

7. Legal Basis for Cookie Use

Under GDPR, our legal basis for using cookies is:

  • Strictly necessary cookies: Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — these are essential to provide the service you request.
  • Analytics, functional, and marketing cookies: Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — we only set these after you give your explicit consent via our cookie banner. You may withdraw consent at any time.
